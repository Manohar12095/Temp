Adding a VTuber Character to Liner — Simple Breakdown
What's the goal?
Make the Liner AI feel more alive by adding an animated character (like a VTuber) that reacts when it's thinking, speaking, or idle.

3 Ways to Do It
Option 1 — Sprite-Based (Easiest)
Swap between a few still images (e.g., mouth open vs. closed) to fake animation. No extra tools needed, but it looks basic.
Option 2 — VTube Studio (Middle ground)
Connect to the VTube Studio app (runs in the background) to drive a real animated character — full facial expressions, physics, smooth movement. Best balance of quality and effort.
Option 3 — Native Live2D SDK (Hardest)
Build the animation engine directly into the app. Looks the most professional, but requires a major rewrite of the UI.

✅ Recommended: Start with Option 1 for a quick result, or go with Option 2 if you want it to look truly polished.
